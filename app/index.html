How to play

Fill in the grid from the clues below. Type a full answer and press Enter — correct letters turn green, right-letter-wrong-spot turns yellow, and letters not in the answer turn gray, same as Wordle. Solve all five visible clues to unlock a sixth bonus entry that closes off the grid. You have 5 wrong guesses shared across the whole puzzle — use them all up before solving everything and the grid reveals as a loss.

Real, full-length answers with one bonus entry that unlocks once the other five are solved. Clues are generated and mechanically verified by the cryptic setter — the LLM only ever writes the surface reading; the wordplay itself is checked by code.
