3A2Dle

A daily five-clue cryptic crossword, built from real, full-length answer words — never the two- and three-letter fill of an ordinary mini. Most days it's three across clues and two down; some days it flips to two across and three down. Either way, a sixth entry runs through the grid without ever getting its own clue — filled in as a side effect of solving the other five, then revealed as a bonus the moment the grid closes.

This screen shows a 3-across / 2-down day: CHESS, ANGLE and REEFS across; CHAIR and EAGLE down; SEEDS is the bonus.
